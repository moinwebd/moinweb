Freelance Website Designer Prices: What Does a Business Website Cost in 2026?

BY MOINUDDIN UPDATED: OCT 2026 10 MIN READ

Quick Answer: Freelance website designer prices for small business projects typically range from $200 to $700 for a complete 3-to-10 page custom WordPress or responsive HTML website. Hourly freelance web designer rates generally fall between $25 and $75+ per hour, while complex eCommerce stores or custom web applications are quoted individually by scope.

How much does a freelance website designer charge? The exact cost depends on your business goals. A fast, single-page landing page or 5-page local service website requires a very different scope than a custom multi-language platform, law firm portal, or online store with booking and payment integrations.

Understanding freelance website designer prices in 2026 helps you plan a realistic budget, compare proposals apples-to-apples, and invest in a high-converting website without overpaying for agency overhead or unnecessary features.

1. How Much Does a Small Business Website Cost in 2026?

The total small business website cost depends on page count, platform choice (such as WordPress or custom semantic HTML5/Tailwind), and whether you need Local SEO setup. Below is a transparent breakdown of typical freelance website design prices by project category:

Project Type What Is Typically Included Typical Freelance Price
Landing Page / Single-Page Site 1 high-converting long-scroll page, lead capture form, mobile optimization, fast load speed $150 – $250
Basic Informational Business Site 3–5 pages (Home, About, Services, Contact), responsive UI/UX, contact form, basic on-page SEO $200 – $350
Custom Small Business / WordPress Site 6–10 pages, custom WordPress theme or frontend build, dedicated service pages, schema markup, Local SEO foundation $350 – $700
Website Redesign & Speed Overhaul Modern UI/UX refresh, Core Web Vitals optimization, sitemap restructuring, 301 redirect mapping $250 – $650+
eCommerce / Custom Portal WooCommerce or custom store, product catalog, payment gateway setup, booking or admission portals $600 – $1,500+ (Quoted by scope)

Note: The ranges above reflect typical freelance web designer fees for small-to-medium businesses and exclude third-party domain registration, web hosting, or paid software licenses.

Basic Informational Website ($200 – $350)

A foundational 3-to-5 page website is ideal for new startups, independent consultants, and local service providers who need a credible online presence that loads fast on mobile devices and captures customer inquiries.

Custom Small Business & WordPress Website ($350 – $700)

Established service businesses—such as law firms, contractors, medical clinics, real estate platforms, and educational institutions—often need 6 to 10+ pages. This tier includes dedicated pages for each core service, portfolio or case study layouts, custom WordPress CMS management, and technical Local SEO so your business ranks in local search results.

Website Redesign & SEO Performance Upgrade ($250 – $650+)

If your current website looks outdated, loads slowly on mobile, or fails to generate leads, a strategic redesign upgrades your visual brand and fixes technical SEO bottlenecks while preserving your existing Google rankings with proper URL redirects.

2. What Determines Freelance Website Designer Prices?

Why does one freelancer quote $250 while another quotes $1,200 for seemingly similar websites? Six core factors influence the final website design cost:

1. Website Size and Cost Per Page

More pages require more wireframing, layout styling, mobile testing, and metadata optimization. While a standard inner page (such as an About or FAQ page) is quick to structure, a custom interactive service page or case study showcase requires significantly more design and frontend engineering time.

2. Custom UI/UX Design vs. Pre-Made Templates

Installing a pre-made WordPress template is faster and cheaper upfront, but bloated multipurpose themes often suffer from slow load speeds and generic layouts. A custom-designed website tailored around your brand identity, typography, and customer conversion flow takes more skill to craft—and delivers much higher long-term ROI.

3. Advanced Website Functionality

Interactive features and custom integrations directly impact freelance web developer rates. Common examples include:

  • Custom multi-step quote or admission inquiry forms.
  • Appointment scheduling and calendar booking systems.
  • Bilingual or multilingual content architectures (e.g., English & Bengali/Spanish).
  • Real estate property filters, searchable directories, or digital book/audio libraries.
  • Online payment gateways (Stripe, PayPal, SSLCommerz) and WooCommerce checkout flows.

4. SEO Copywriting and Visual Asset Preparation

A beautiful layout cannot convert visitors without clear, persuasive copywriting. If you supply organized text and high-resolution photos, your design cost stays lower. If you need your freelancer to research keywords, write service-page copy, compress images to WebP format, and design custom graphics, verify whether content creation is included in the quote.

5. On-Page & Technical Local SEO Inclusion

Many cheap web design quotes deliver a site that looks fine visually but is invisible to Google—missing `H1/H2` hierarchy, meta descriptions, `JSON-LD` Schema markup, XML sitemaps, and Core Web Vitals optimization. Hiring a freelancer who builds SEO-friendly architecture from day one saves you from paying for an expensive SEO overhaul later.

3. Common Freelance Website Designer Pricing Models

Freelance web designers generally bill in one of three ways: fixed-price per project, hourly rates, or monthly retainers.

Pricing Model Typical 2026 Rate Best For Question to Ask
Fixed-Price Project $200 – $1,000+ per site Full website builds & redesigns with a clear page list "What exact deliverables and revision rounds are included?"
Hourly Rate $25 – $75+ / hour Custom feature additions, bug fixes, or evolving scope "How many hours do you estimate for this milestone?"
Monthly Retainer $75 – $300+ / month Ongoing Local SEO, content updates, backups & speed care "What monthly tasks and response times are guaranteed?"

Fixed-Price Website Design (Recommended for Most Small Businesses)

With fixed-price billing, you know the exact total investment before work begins. It works best when both you and the designer agree on a written list of pages, features, revision rounds, and milestone payment stages (for example, 50% to start and 50% upon completion).

Hourly Freelance Web Designer Rates

Hourly billing is ideal when you already have a live website and need specific frontend improvements, speed optimization, landing page additions, or technical SEO fixes. Always ask for an estimated hour range before approving work.

5. Hidden & Recurring Website Costs to Keep in Mind

The one-time web design fee is only part of owning a business website. To avoid surprises after launch, factor these standard recurring or optional costs into your annual budget:

Expense Item Typical Cost Frequency What to Confirm With Your Designer
Domain Name (.com, .info, .law, etc.) $10 – $20 / year Yearly Ensure the domain is registered in your own name.
Web Hosting & SSL Certificate $40 – $120 / year (or $0 on static cloud hosts) Yearly / Monthly Confirm free Let's Encrypt SSL (HTTPS) is included.
Premium Plugins or Themes $0 – $100 / year Optional / Yearly Ask if lightweight free or custom code alternatives exist.
Ongoing Local SEO & Content $100 – $400+ / month Optional Monthly Needed if you want continuous blog posts & local ranking growth.
Maintenance & Security Backups $30 – $100 / month Optional WordPress sites need periodic updates; static HTML sites need almost zero maintenance.

6. How to Budget Smartly Without Sacrificing Quality

  1. Start With High-Impact Core Pages: Launch with a strong Homepage, About, Core Services, Portfolio/Reviews, and Contact page ($200–$500). You can always add extra city pages or blog articles later as revenue grows.
  2. Prepare Your Text and Photos Early: Gathering your service descriptions, team photos, and customer testimonials before the project starts prevents delays and keeps design costs low.
  3. Avoid Bloated Paid Plugins: Clean semantic HTML/Tailwind or lightweight custom WordPress builds run faster, rank higher on Google, and save you hundreds of dollars in annual plugin renewal fees.
  4. Invest in Foundational Local SEO Upfront: Making sure your title tags, schema markup, and mobile speed are properly engineered during the build is far cheaper than hiring an SEO consultant to fix a broken site later.

7. Questions to Ask Before Accepting a Web Design Quote

  • Is this quote a fixed total price or an hourly estimate?
  • How many pages and revision rounds are included in this price?
  • Will the website pass Google Core Web Vitals and mobile usability checks?
  • Is basic on-page SEO (meta tags, heading structure, XML sitemap, schema) included?
  • Do I get 100% ownership of the source code, WordPress admin, and domain after final payment?
  • What post-launch support or training is included if I need to edit text or images?

9. Frequently Asked Questions About Freelance Website Designer Prices

How much does a freelance website designer charge in 2026? ▼

For most small business websites (3 to 10 pages), a freelance website designer typically charges between $200 and $700 as a fixed project fee, or $25 to $75+ per hour depending on custom UI/UX, WordPress development, and Local SEO requirements.

What is included in a website design cost? ▼

Depending on the agreement, the cost typically includes sitemap planning, custom UI/UX design, mobile-responsive development, contact forms, basic on-page SEO preparation, speed optimization, and launch support. Domain, hosting, copywriting, and ongoing maintenance may be billed separately.

How much does a WordPress website cost with a freelancer? ▼

A custom, speed-optimized WordPress website for a small business typically costs between $250 and $700 with an experienced freelancer, whereas large multi-page or WooCommerce stores are quoted individually based on product and integration needs.

Is hourly or fixed-price website design better? ▼

Fixed-price quotes are best when your page count and features are clearly defined upfront. Hourly pricing is better for ongoing tweaks, troubleshooting, or evolving scopes.

Are website hosting and domain registration included in the design fee? ▼

Not always. Some designers include initial setup, while others recommend clients purchase hosting and domain services directly so the business retains 100% account ownership.

Does website design include Local SEO? ▼

Standard web design usually includes foundational on-page SEO (meta titles, headings, mobile speed, and schema markup). Comprehensive Local SEO—such as Google Business Profile optimization, city landing pages, and ongoing keyword ranking—is typically offered as an add-on or dedicated package.

Why is a freelance web designer more affordable than an agency? ▼

Web design agencies often charge $2,500 to $10,000+ because of office overhead, account managers, and layered teams. Working directly with a skilled freelancer eliminates agency overhead while delivering personalized design, clean code, and direct communication.

Is a cheap website designer worth it? ▼

An affordable price ($200–$500) is very reasonable for a well-scoped small business project, provided the quote includes mobile responsiveness, clean on-page SEO, clear revision limits, and full file ownership.
