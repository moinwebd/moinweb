07 / FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about website cost, Local SEO timelines and how I work.

How much does a website cost?+

Freelance website designer prices for small business projects typically range from $200 to $700, depending on page count, WordPress or custom design and Local SEO needs. You get a written quote with a clear scope before any work starts.

How long does Local SEO take?+

Most local businesses see early movement in Google Maps and local search within 4 to 8 weeks, with steadier gains over 3 to 6 months. Timing depends on competition, your Google Business Profile and the reviews and citations you already have.

Do you build websites on WordPress?+

Yes. I build WordPress websites that are fast, mobile-friendly and easy for you to edit, and you keep full ownership of all files.

Which countries do you work in?+

I work remotely with small businesses in the US, UK, Canada and Australia, and I'm happy to take on clients elsewhere. Everything runs online through email, chat and video calls.

What do I get in the free audit?+

A short review of your current website and Google Business Profile: mobile usability, page speed, local SEO basics and the quickest fixes likely to bring more calls.

Can you help my business rank on Google Maps?+

Yes. I optimize your Google Business Profile, add dedicated service-area pages and LocalBusiness schema, and make your site clear about the services and areas you cover. No one can guarantee a number one position, but these are the steps that move rankings.
